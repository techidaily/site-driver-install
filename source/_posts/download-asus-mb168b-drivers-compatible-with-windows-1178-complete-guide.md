1\. Go to[ASUS website](https://www.asus.com).

![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/04/img_58f5881487021.png)

![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/04/img_58f58b42b3b31.png)
