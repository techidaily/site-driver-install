Here are the steps:

1. Go to [Gaomon’s website](https://www.gaomon.net/) .
2. Select**Products** \>**Pen Tablet.**  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/manual-update-1.jpg)
3. Scroll down to click**S620** .
