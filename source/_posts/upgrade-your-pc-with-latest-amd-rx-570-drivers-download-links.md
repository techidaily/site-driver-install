1. Go to **[AMD’s support website](https://www.amd.com/en/support)**  .
2. Click**Graphics** \>**Raden 500 Series** \>**Raden RX 500 Series** \>**Raden RX 570** .  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/1-1-7.jpg)
